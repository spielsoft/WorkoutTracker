Focused gym logging

Log the set.
Keep the sheet.

WorkoutTracker turns a user-owned Google Sheet into a fast, purpose-built workout logger for iPhone and Mac. Your program stays readable, editable, and yours.

Designed for the set in front of you

Less app between you and the workout.

Select or create your own Google Sheet, organize exercises into workouts, and record results in a compact interface. WorkoutTracker handles the workflow while the Sheet remains the durable record.

01

Your data stays useful

Workout rows, targets, notes, and history remain visible in a normal Google Sheet that you can inspect and edit directly.

02

Fast set entry

Row-specific formats become focused entry fields, with the last result ready as the starting point for the next set.

03

Safe by default

The app validates workbook structure before it writes and keeps unrecognized set text intact instead of silently discarding it.

A short path to the first set

Your Google Sheet, with a gym-ready front end.

WorkoutTracker uses native Google Sign-In so you can select an existing workout Sheet or create one of your own, then get straight to logging.

1

Connect Google

Sign in and grant the Sheet permissions the app needs.

2

Select your Sheet

Choose a workout Sheet you own or create a fresh one.

3

Train and log

Record sets quickly and see the results in Google Sheets.

A deliberately small data footprint

No account database. No ads. No analytics SDK.

WorkoutTracker communicates directly with Google APIs from your device. Durable workout data stays in your Sheet, while limited workspace preferences stay locally on your device.
