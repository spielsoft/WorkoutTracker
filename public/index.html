WorkoutTracker Privacy Policy

Fast workout logging over your own spreadsheet.

WorkoutTracker is a lightweight gym logging app for people who want a focused phone and desktop interface without giving up their human-readable workout sheet.

Durable workout data is stored in a user-owned Google Sheet in the user's Google Drive. WorkoutTracker does not run a workout data server and does not create a separate WorkoutTracker database.

What It Does

After native Google Sign-In, the app uses a custom in-app chooser to find or create a workout spreadsheet. It validates the sheet, presents workouts and history, and writes compact set notation back to visible cells.

Your Sheet

The Google Sheet remains the source of truth. You can open and edit it directly in Google Sheets when the app is unavailable.

Support

Email WorkoutTracker support to request help or report a problem. Include your platform, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send Google credentials or private workout data.

Data Model

  • Workout rows and history live in the first tab of the selected spreadsheet.
  • Canonical exercise metadata lives in the sheet's Exercises tab.
  • Unparseable set cells are preserved as text instead of discarded.
  • Firebase Hosting serves only these static support and privacy pages. The app communicates directly with Google APIs from your device.

Read the WorkoutTracker Privacy Policy for details about Google authorization and data storage.
