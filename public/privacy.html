WorkoutTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026

WorkoutTracker stores durable workout data in a user-owned Google Sheet, not in a WorkoutTracker server or separate WorkoutTracker database.

Overview

WorkoutTracker is a gym workout logging app that uses Google authorization so you can select, create, read, and write a workout spreadsheet in your Google Drive. The selected Google Sheet is the durable record for your workout data.

Data You Store

Workout data, exercise names, workout targets, notes, and set history are stored in the Google Sheet you choose or create. WorkoutTracker does not copy that workout history into an app-operated cloud database.

Google Authorization

The app requests Google authorization to let you sign in, choose or create a spreadsheet, validate the sheet layout, and read or write the selected workout spreadsheet. Google may show your account identity during sign-in so you can confirm the account you are using.

What WorkoutTracker Does Not Operate

  • No WorkoutTracker account database is created for you.
  • No WorkoutTracker server stores your durable workout history.
  • No Firebase Realtime Database, Firestore, Storage, or Analytics SDK is loaded by these public pages.
  • Firebase Hosting is used only to publish static support and privacy pages.

Your Control

Because the Google Sheet is owned by your Google account, you can view, edit, export, share, or delete it through Google Drive and Google Sheets. Removing app access in your Google Account stops future app access to your Google data, subject to Google's account and Drive behavior.

Support

For privacy or support questions, use the contact channel from the store listing or distribution page where you received WorkoutTracker.
