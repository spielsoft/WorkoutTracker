Privacy Policy

Your workout belongs in a Sheet you own—not in a WorkoutTracker database. This policy explains the limited information the app uses to connect your device to Google Sheets.

Effective date: July 11, 2026

In short: WorkoutTracker has no workout-data backend, advertising network, or analytics SDK. Durable workout data stays in your user-owned Google Sheet. Limited workspace preferences stay on your device.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the WorkoutTracker applications for iOS and macOS and to these public support pages. WorkoutTracker lets you sign in with Google, select or create your own Google Sheet, organize your workouts, and write workout results back to that Sheet.

2. Information the App Processes

  • Google account identity. Native Google Sign-In may provide your email address, display name, and profile photo. The app uses these details to show the connected account and keep a saved Sheet bound to the account that selected it.
  • Google Sheet metadata. The app reads limited Drive metadata such as Sheet names, identifiers, links, and modification times so you can find and select a Sheet.
  • Google Sheet content. The app reads and writes the selected Sheet to validate its structure, show workouts and exercises, create visible history, and apply changes you request.
  • Local app state. The app may store the selected Sheet's ID, name, link, account email, last entered Sheet identifier, and recent workout or history choices in the platform's application support storage.
  • Store purchase information. If you obtain the app through the Apple App Store or another app store, that store handles payment and purchase records under its own privacy policy. WorkoutTracker does not receive your full payment-card details.

3. Google Permissions

WorkoutTracker currently requests these Google authorization scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly allows the app to find Google Sheets and read their file metadata. It does not grant general Drive file-content access.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets allows the app to create, validate, read, and write spreadsheet content needed for workout setup and logging.

Authentication credentials are handled by Google Sign-In and the platform's protected storage. Do not send credentials or authorization tokens to WorkoutTracker support.

4. How Information Is Used and Shared

WorkoutTracker uses account identity to display and preserve the active account, Sheet metadata to help you choose a workbook, and Sheet content to perform the workout actions you request. The app communicates directly with Google APIs from your device.

WorkoutTracker does not sell personal information, use workout data for advertising, or send workout data to a maintainer-operated backend. Information may be processed by Google when you use Google Sign-In, Drive, or Sheets, and by an app store when it distributes or sells the app, under those providers' terms and privacy policies.

5. Hosted Pages

Firebase Hosting serves the static homepage, Privacy Policy, and proposed Terms. These pages do not load Firebase Authentication, Analytics, a database, cloud storage, advertising code, or a Google sign-in flow. The hosting provider may process ordinary network request information needed to deliver and protect the pages.

6. Retention and Your Controls

  • Your Google Sheet remains under your Google account's control. You can view, edit, export, share, or delete it in Google Drive or Google Sheets at any time.
  • Signing out of WorkoutTracker clears its saved Google workspace selection. Removing the app clears app-local data according to the operating system's behavior. Neither action deletes your Sheet.
  • You can revoke WorkoutTracker's access in your Google Account's third-party access settings. Revocation stops future authorized requests but does not delete your Sheet.

7. Security and Your Responsibilities

WorkoutTracker validates workbook structure before app-directed writes and preserves set text it cannot parse. No software can guarantee absolute security or prevent every spreadsheet mistake. Protect your device and Google account, review sharing permissions, and keep backups appropriate for your needs.

8. Children

WorkoutTracker is a general fitness logging tool and is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise a minor's use where required by applicable law.

9. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as the app, its permissions, or its distribution changes. The effective date above will change when a revised policy is published.

For privacy questions, access requests, or support, email ian.b.spielman@gmail.com. Do not include Google credentials or private workout data.
