WorkoutTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

WorkoutTracker stores durable workout data in a user-owned Google Sheet, while limited app state is stored locally on your device. The app communicates directly with Google APIs.

Overview

WorkoutTracker uses native Google Sign-In for account access. Its custom in-app chooser uses the Google Drive API to discover Google Sheets. The app then uses the Google Sheets API to create, validate, read, and write the Sheet you choose. This chooser runs in the app; these hosted pages do not participate in sign-in or sheet selection.

Data and Storage

  • Google account identity: Google Sign-In provides your email address and may provide your display name and profile photo so the app can show and bind the active account.
  • Local app state: the app stores application state in its platform application-support directory. This can include the selected Sheet's ID, name, link, account email, the last-entered Sheet identifier or link, and the last workout and history choices.
  • User-owned Google Sheet: workout data, exercise names, targets, notes, and set history remain in the Sheet you choose or create in your Google Drive. That Sheet is the durable source of truth.
  • Hosted pages: Firebase Hosting publishes only these static support and privacy pages. The pages do not load a Firebase database, storage, authentication, or analytics client.

Google Authorization Scopes

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly lets the custom chooser discover recent or searched Google Sheets and read their file metadata. It does not grant Drive file-content access.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets lets the app create, validate, read and write spreadsheet content needed for workout authoring and logging.

How the App Uses Data

Account identity is used to display the signed-in account and prevent a saved Sheet from silently moving to another account. Sheet metadata is used to present chooser results. Spreadsheet content is used to validate the workbook, show workouts and history, and apply edits you request. WorkoutTracker does not use this data for advertising or sell it. The project maintainer does not receive it through these hosted pages.

Deletion and Revocation

  • Sign out in WorkoutTracker to clear its saved workspace selection and locally cached workspace state.
  • View, edit, export, share, or delete your user-owned Google Sheet in Google Drive or Google Sheets. Deleting app-local state does not delete the Sheet.
  • Revoke WorkoutTracker's Google access from the third-party access controls in your Google Account. Revocation stops future authorized app requests but does not delete your Sheet or other Google data.
